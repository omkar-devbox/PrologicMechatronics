Our Solutions

Comprehensive Material Handling &
Automation Solutions

End-to-end engineered systems for efficient, safe, and flexible manufacturing operations across automotive, logistics, and industrial facilities.

Floor Conveyors

(Slat, Roller, Belt & Towline)

Roller, Belt, Chain, Towline and Slat Conveyors for efficient material movement in assembly lines.

EMS & EBS

(Monorail Bi-rail System)

Overhead conveyor systems for automotive assembly, body shop lines, and high-speed material transport.

Overhead Forged Chain

(High Temp Oven & Paint Shop)

Drop-forged rivetless chain conveyors engineered for high-temperature ovens up to 250°C and heavy loads.

Material Transfer

(Transfer Car, Turn Table, POP Up)

Flexible solutions for lateral shuttling, 90°/180° rotational turning, and vertical pop-up transfer.

Lifting Systems

(Elevators, Lifters, Synchronizers)

Vertical material movement solutions (VRC), multi-level elevators, and synchronized multi-axis carriage lifters.

ASRS Systems

(Automated Storage & Retrieval)

Automated stacker cranes and high-density racking with WMS integration for high-density smart warehousing.

Electrical & Control

(PLC, SCADA, HMI, Industry 4.0)

PLC, HMI, SCADA based electrical control automation with CPRI panels and Industry 4.0 factory upgrades.

Flexible Conveyors

(Expandable & Telescopic)

Adaptable and space-efficient scissor expandable roller conveyors for dynamic warehouse truck loading.

JIT Delivery Systems

(Lineside Parts Sequencing)

Integrated automation with Pick to Light to ensure the right material, at the right time, with zero error.
