Engineering FAQ

Frequently Asked Technical Inquiries

Key information regarding turnkey proposals, timelines, and trial visits.

What information is needed for an initial quotation? ↓

To formulate a preliminary layout proposal, we typically request component payload & dimensions, target line cycle time, factory floor layout (PDF/DWG), and desired PLC architecture (Siemens / Rockwell).

What are standard lead times for custom conveyors? ↓

Standard modular conveyor units ship within 3 to 6 weeks. Larger turnkey EMS/EBS monorails, multi-level ASRS stackers, or synchronized body assembly lines typically span 10 to 18 weeks including FAT trials.

Do you provide complete erection and commissioning? ↓

Yes, our site engineering crew handles mechanical installation, control panel integration, sensor cabling, trial batch production, and operator handover across India.

Can we schedule an in-person factory visit to witness trial runs? ↓

Clients are always welcome at our works in Kuruli, Pune. You can inspect running test tracks, panel fabrication labs, and review CAD models directly with our design engineering team.
